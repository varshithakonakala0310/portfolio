Flagship  ·  MSc Dissertation

Business Analytics & Decision-Making

“A Study of How UK Businesses Use Data in Strategic Planning”

My dissertation asked a practical question: can business analytics genuinely improve strategic decision-making in UK small and medium-sized enterprises? Using financial records for 248 UK SMEs (January 2018 – February 2023), I built a three-stage analysis: descriptive analytics, correlation and regression, then predictive modelling.

Regression explained more than 70% of the variance in growth outcomes in the original study, and explainability analysis with SHAP showed that profitability efficiency, especially profit per employee and profit margin, drives growth outlooks.

Models compared

  • Random Forest
  • Gradient Boosting
  • XGBoost — best in the original study
  • Regression

Strongest predictors

  • Profit per employee
  • Profit margin
